About Our School

Discover the rich heritage and values that define Kenyatta High School Mwatate.

History & Heritage

Kenyatta High School Mwatate was founded on August 7, 1970 by Kenya's first president, Mzee Jomo Kenyatta. The school started as a community initiative to provide secondary education to boys in the Taita region. It was officially opened with three streams under the leadership of its first principal, Hon. Eliud Mcharo.

Over the decades, the school has expanded to four streams and currently hosts over 950 students. Operating under the motto "Education for service", Kenyatta High Mwatate remains a top-performing national school in the Coast region.

Our Mission

To provide a holistic education that empowers learners to achieve academic excellence and become responsible, morally upright citizens.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of excellence in secondary education, nurturing innovative and morally upright leaders.
